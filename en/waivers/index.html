What is the waiver service?

An immigration waiver is a legal request asking the U.S. government to forgive specific violations or inadmissibility grounds that could block a visa or residency application. It offers a chance to move forward legally.

Our consultants prepare and submit waiver forms such as I-601, I-601A, or I-212, depending on your case. We ensure all evidence, affidavits, and supporting documents clearly demonstrate hardship and eligibility.

Having professional guidance is crucial, as a well-structured waiver can make the difference between approval and denial of your immigration request.

FAQ

Here are the most common questions about immigration waivers in Florida, answered clearly to help you understand the process.

Who can apply for an immigration waiver?

Anyone facing visa or green card denial due to inadmissibility issues, unlawful presence, or previous violations may qualify for a waiver.

What are the main types of waivers?

The most common are I-601 for general inadmissibility, I-601A for provisional unlawful presence, and I-212 for reentry after deportation.

How long does it take to process a waiver?

Processing times vary, but most waivers take between 6 and 12 months, depending on USCIS or consulate workload.

Can a waiver guarantee approval of my case?

No, but a well-prepared waiver significantly increases the likelihood of approval and speeds up your immigration process.

What documents are required for a waiver?

Evidence of hardship, identity documents, financial records, medical reports, and personal statements are commonly required.

Do I need to attend an interview?

Some waivers require interviews, especially if connected to consular processing. Our team prepares you for every possible question.

Can I work or travel while waiting for a waiver decision?

It depends on your status. Certain applicants may qualify for temporary work permits while waiting for a decision.

Is hiring a professional necessary?

Yes, immigration waivers involve complex legal arguments. Professional help ensures accuracy and strengthens your case for approval.
