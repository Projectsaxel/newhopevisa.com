1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device when you visit a website. They help the site function, remember preferences, and understand how visitors use pages.

2. Types of Cookies We May Use

  • Essential cookies: required for basic operation and security.
  • Performance and analytics cookies: help us understand visits, pages viewed, and interactions (e.g., Google Analytics / Site Kit).
  • Functionality cookies: remember preferences and improve browsing experience.
  • Marketing cookies: may be used to measure campaigns or promotional content, when applicable.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by third-party services integrated into the site, such as analytics, maps, videos, or social networks. Those providers have their own privacy and cookie policies.

4. Legal Basis and Consent

Where required by law, we will request your consent before using non-essential cookies. You may withdraw or change consent at any time through browser settings or available site controls, when provided.

5. Managing Cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect how parts of the site work.

6. Retention

Cookies may remain on your device for varying periods—from session cookies (until you close the browser) to months, depending on purpose and type.
